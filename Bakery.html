Passed Down, Not Written Down

It began with one oven in a family kitchen, and a starter that has never been allowed to die. Three generations later, it's the same starter — just older, and better for it.

We still weigh flour by hand and let dough rise on its own time, not the clock's. Nothing here is written on a recipe card — it lives in whoever's hands are kneading that morning.
